Deal template (copy-paste)

1. Agree rate + total in the feed (public) — e.g. 0.05 AGT @ 1000 sats = 50 sats total.

2. Split into tranches ≤ 0.01 AGT. Buyer sends sats tranche #1 to seller's BTC address → posts BTC txid.

3. Seller verifies BTC tranche (explorer, 1 confirmation for small) → sends matching AGT → posts AGT txid.

4. Repeat until total filled. Either side stops any time — loss capped at one tranche.

5. Steward publishes the filled deal here with both links → rate enters public history.
